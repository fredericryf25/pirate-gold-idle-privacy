Privacy Policy – Pirate Gold Idle

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pirate Gold Idle (the "Game"), developed by Fred Games ("we", "us"), handles information when you play the Game on Android.

1. Summary

  • The Game has no account system. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other directly identifying information.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • The Game uses Google services for ads (Google AdMob), in-app purchases (Google Play Billing) and usage statistics (Google Firebase Analytics). These services may collect device identifiers and usage data as described below.

2. Information stored on your device

Your progress (coins, crew, islands, settings, achievements, voyages, purchases unlocked in the Game) is stored in a local save file on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the Game deletes this file.

The Game may schedule local notifications (for example "Your ship is back!" or a daily gift reminder). They are created on your device and do not send any data. You can turn them off in the Game settings or in your Android settings.

3. Third-party services

Google AdMob (advertising). The Game shows optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device information (model, OS version, language) and ad interactions, in order to deliver, measure and personalize ads and to prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Google Play Billing (purchases). In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment card or bank details. We only receive a confirmation of the purchase so the Game can unlock the item. Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Google Firebase Analytics (statistics). We use Firebase Analytics to understand how players use the Game (for example which islands are reached, sessions and play time) so we can improve it. Firebase may collect an app instance identifier, device information, approximate country, and in-game events. This data is used in aggregated form. Data is kept for up to 14 months. Privacy and Security in Firebase.

The Google Privacy Policy applies to these services: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. How we use information

  • To run the Game and save your progress (on your device).
  • To show optional rewarded ads and keep the Game free.
  • To deliver in-app purchases.
  • To analyze aggregated usage and improve the Game.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Your choices

  • Ads personalization: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads). In the EEA/UK you can change your consent from the consent form.
  • Notifications: turn them off in the Game settings or in Android settings.
  • Delete your data: uninstall the Game to delete your local save. To ask for deletion of analytics data linked to your device, contact us (see below) with your request.

6. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Security

Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit. No system is 100% secure, but we only use reputable providers and keep the data we handle to a minimum.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be announced in the Game or on its store page.
